Privacy & Data Policy

How your game data is processed and how you can manage your choices.

1. Scope and contact

This policy applies to the RUNVOK Android game developed by Duvar Mobile and its support services. For privacy questions or requests, contact duvarmobile@gmail.com.

This policy describes local game saves, Google Mobile Ads rewarded test ads, server time, and optional bug reports in RUNVOK v0.8.9. Duvar Mobile account creation, Google Play Games sign-in, game-managed cloud saves, and real-money purchases are not active in this version. We will update this policy when those features change.

2. Game data stored on your device

Game progress, your chosen player name and character, profile preferences, race and tournament results, missions, in-game balances, and settings are stored on your device. In the current version, they are not automatically uploaded to a Duvar Mobile account or a game-managed cloud save service. Android system backup may separately back up app data depending on your device and Google Account settings; you can manage this through Android/Google backup settings.

Life, wheel, and mission timers use server time and time elapsed since the device started, rather than the date and time you can manually change on your phone. A hash of the Android identifier and the device boot count are stored locally to distinguish device restarts. These values are not sent with time requests.

3. Server time

When needed at game launch or after a device restart, the game connects to the time service over HTTPS. The request contains a random, temporary value used to match the response to that request. Your player name, progress, and balances are not part of the time request.

As part of providing internet services, hosting and security providers may process your IP address, connection time, and basic request information. The time service does not use this information to build player profiles or target advertising.

4. Optional bug reports and support

If you use “Report a bug,” your description, game version, device model, operating system, client-side player identifier, random installation identifier, and report identifier are sent to the support server. Do not include passwords, payment details, or unnecessary personal information in your description.

We process this information to investigate issues, prevent duplicate submissions, and limit misuse. The server uses keyed hashes of the installation identifier and IP address. Report content is stored in a support record and, when the email service is enabled, is forwarded only to duvarmobile@gmail.com. No screenshot, photo, contact list, or location is automatically attached.

If submission fails, the draft remains on your device and is not shown as successfully sent. If you contact us directly by email, we also process your email address and the information in your message to handle your support request.

5. Service providers and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We use GitHub Pages for this website, OpenAI Sites and Cloudflare infrastructure for time and report services, Google AdMob for rewarded test ads, and Google Gmail when support email forwarding is enabled. These providers may process connection and service data under their own privacy policies, including on servers outside your country.

6. Advertising and purchases

RUNVOK contains rewarded advertising provided through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Players choose whether to watch a rewarded ad. The v0.8.9 test build uses Google's official rewarded test ad unit; these test ads do not generate advertising revenue. The game does not show banner ads or forced interstitial ads. An ad reward is granted only after the ad network reports completion.

Using test ads does not mean that the advertising SDK processes no data. The SDK initializes when the game starts. According to Google's disclosure, it may automatically collect and share with Google your IP address (which can be used to estimate approximate location), interactions such as app launches, taps, and video views, performance and diagnostic information, and device identifiers such as the advertising ID and app set ID. These data are used for advertising delivery and measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. Choosing not to watch an ad does not necessarily stop all data processing during SDK initialization. SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID using Android's advertising privacy settings. This does not stop all technical data processing. For choices and retention practices relating to data processed by Google, see the Google Privacy Policy, My Ad Center, and Google Mobile Ads data disclosure.

Real-money purchases cannot be initiated in the current version. The game does not request card numbers, card security codes, or payment account details. Before enabling real Google Play payments or revenue-generating ads, we will implement the relevant data flows and required consent choices and update this policy.

7. Retention and deletion

You can delete local game data by clearing the app's storage in Android settings. This removes your game progress. Support records and emails that have reached the server are separate from local app data.

Completed server reports have a 90-day cleanup threshold: when a new submission succeeds, completed records older than this threshold are deleted. Deletion may therefore occur after, rather than exactly on, day 90. Reports with uncertain delivery or unresolved issues are kept until their investigation is complete. Support emails are retained for as long as necessary to respond to your request, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. Providers' technical logs are subject to their own retention policies.

8. Your choices and data deletion requests

RUNVOK data deletion request: Email duvarmobile@gmail.com with the subject “RUNVOK data deletion request.” If available, include your report number and identify the support record you want deleted. Do not send passwords or payment details. After verifying your request, we will delete or de-identify the relevant report text, associated device/installation information, and support correspondence under our control. Records needed for security, disputes, or legal obligations may be retained only as long as necessary for those purposes; we will explain any such exception in our response.

To delete game progress on your device, go to Android Settings → Apps → RUNVOK → Storage → Clear data. To manage Google advertising data, use the Google links above and Android advertising privacy settings. This version does not have a Duvar Mobile user account to delete.

Submitting a bug report is optional. Depending on the rules where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your information. Send requests to duvarmobile@gmail.com, preferably with the relevant report number. We will request only the additional information necessary to verify your request.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has shared personal information through support, contact us so we can investigate and delete it when appropriate.

9. Security and changes

Time and report connections use HTTPS, and Google Mobile Ads data is transferred using TLS. Email authorization credentials are held on the server, not included in the game package. No electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

We will update this policy and its revision date when game features or data processing practices change. Where required, we will also announce significant changes in the app.
